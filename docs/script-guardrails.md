# Script guardrails

Every session script must pass these rules before it is voiced, and again before it is published. They come from research into guided-imagery evidence, meditation adverse effects and trauma-sensitive practice. Anxious listeners are the most likely to be harmed by meditation, so the rules err on the side of gentleness.

## Imagery
- **Use:** daylight or soft light, the shoreline and land, gentle shallows, open huts, paths with visible exits. Each place keeps fixed sensory details from the world bible.
- **Never use:** submersion, sinking, deep or dark water, storms or rip tides; caves, tunnels, small rooms, darkness without light; heights and cliff edges; being stranded, lost or having "no way off"; heaviness or "can't move"; floating out of the body, dissolving, "no longer separate"; sudden noises, threats, injury, illness, death or abandonment.
- **Body and breath:** no heartbeat focus, breath-holding, breath counting, or long scans of the chest, throat or abdomen.
- **Memories:** no recalling painful memories, "inner child" work or "sit with your fear".

## Language
- Second person, present tense, UK spelling.
- Invitational: "you might", "if it feels okay", "or simply".
- Never: "close your eyes", "breathe deeply", "relax now", "let go completely", "you are completely safe", or promised outcomes.
- Mind-wandering and mixed experiences are normal. Not relaxing is never framed as failure.

## Structure (every session)
1. Opening choice: eyes open or closed, and an anchor.
2. Permission to pause, open the eyes or stop, at the start **and** midway.
3. Arrival → deepening → journey → integration → gradual return.
4. Closing grounding: feet or seat, three sounds in the room, fingers and toes, open the eyes in your own time.
5. A "not while driving" notice.

## Pacing
- Word budget = speech minutes × 105.
- Pauses only as `[PAUSE n s]` and `[SILENCE n s]` tokens. Silences are added when the audio is assembled, not with punctuation or voice tags. Maximum silence length is set per curriculum phase.

## Claims
No "treat", "cure", "reduce anxiety", "therapy", "CBT", "clinically proven", named disorders or time-bound outcomes. This applies to scripts, episode notes, image alt text and social copy. Signposting text for people who need support is written and maintained by a person, never generated.

## Quality passes
Outline (against phase settings) → draft → critic check (world-bible continuity, similarity to recent scripts, clichés, word budget, safety, claims) → revise → **human approval before voicing, and again before publishing**.

## Audio and images
- Same voice model, settings and seed for every session. Listen through for artefacts and tags read aloud.
- Final mix at −16 LUFS / −1 dBTP.
- Images are checked against the place description. No faces. Every image carries an AI-generated credit.
