# The 100-day curriculum map

One session about a week, over 100 days. Day 1 is Monday 16 November 2026. Length is set by the decision on #10: phase 1 starts at 8 minutes and is capped at 12. Word budget = speech minutes × 105 (see `docs/script-guardrails.md`). Source of truth: `content/curriculum.json`; `tests/curriculum.test.ts` checks it.

## Phases

| Phase | Days | Practises | Length | Max silence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Arrival | 1–42 | Settling in: choosing eyes open or closed, one anchor, and getting to know the island's gentlest places by daylight. | 8–12 min | 10 s |
| 2. Wandering | 43–70 | Moving slowly along the island's paths, with more room to notice sounds, textures and light. | 10–14 min | 20 s |
| 3. Belonging | 71–91 | Sharing the island with its people and its high, open places, with longer pauses to simply be there. | 12–16 min | 30 s |
| 4. Perspective | 92–100 | Looking back over the whole island from its quietest places, with the longest pauses. | 14–18 min | 40 s |
