## The East Shore

A long, sheltered beach facing the morning sun, with shallow, clear water and soft sand.

### First Light Beach

A broad curve of pale sand where the morning sun first reaches the island. The sea here is calm and shallow, and the tide slips in and out in long, quiet ripples. A wooden walkway leads back up to the garden path whenever you wish to return.

- **Sights:** pale sand; small, clear ripples; a line of driftwood bleached silver; the sun rising low over the water
- **Sounds:** unhurried waves folding onto sand; distant gulls; the soft hush of foam
- **Textures:** cool, damp sand near the water; warm, dry sand higher up; smooth, worn driftwood
- **Smells:** clean salt air; warm sand
- **Time of day:** early morning
- **Light:** soft golden light with a pale sky
- **Way in:** A wooden walkway from the garden path, with a handrail on one side.
- **Way out:** The same walkway, always in sight, and the open beach stretching both ways.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2

### The Glass Shallows

A wide, clear pool of water that only reaches the ankles on the sand, warmed by the sun. Small, bright shells sit on the bottom and you can see every one. You might paddle, or simply sit on a flat rock at the water's side.

- **Sights:** clear, still water; rippled sand beneath it; small spiral shells; a flat, sun-warmed rock
- **Sounds:** soft trickles where the pool meets the sea; a light breeze
- **Textures:** warm water around the ankles; fine, rippled sand; smooth stone
- **Smells:** warm salt; sun on stone
- **Time of day:** mid-morning
- **Light:** bright, clear daylight
- **Way in:** A short stretch of firm, flat sand from First Light Beach.
- **Way out:** Dry sand begins a few steps away on every side.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2, 3

### The Driftwood Bench

A long bench made of silvered driftwood, set on the dry sand at the top of the beach and facing the sea. A tuft of beach grass leans beside it. It is a place for sitting and noticing the day go by.

- **Sights:** silvered wood grain; beach grass moving gently; a wide, calm horizon
- **Sounds:** waves in the distance; wind through grass
- **Textures:** smooth, sun-warmed wood; dry, fine sand underfoot
- **Smells:** salt; dry grass
- **Time of day:** late morning
- **Light:** bright, even daylight
- **Way in:** A trodden path of flattened grass from the walkway.
- **Way out:** The walkway is a few steps behind the bench, in clear view.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2

### Lantern Cove

A small, open bay at the south end of the beach, where fishing boats rest on the sand in the afternoon light. Strings of paper lanterns hang along the harbour wall, lit softly even by day. The bay is wide and open to the sea, with the beach in view all the way round.

- **Sights:** painted boats resting on sand; paper lanterns; a low stone wall; wide open water
- **Sounds:** rope creaking softly; water lapping the hulls
- **Textures:** weathered painted wood; smooth, warm stone
- **Smells:** tar and rope; sea air
- **Time of day:** mid-afternoon
- **Light:** warm, soft afternoon light
- **Way in:** A sandy path along the water's side from the Driftwood Bench.
- **Way out:** The sandy path, plus steps up to the harbour wall.
- **Suits phases:** 2, 3

## The Garden Terraces

Gentle, low terraces of herbs, orchards and flowers behind the beach, linked by wide paths.

### The Herb Terrace

A low, sunny terrace of rosemary, thyme and lavender in neat, raised beds. Bees move slowly from flower to flower. A wide stone path runs between the beds, with a wooden seat at one end.

- **Sights:** purple lavender; grey-green rosemary; slow bees; a wooden seat
- **Sounds:** quiet humming of bees; a light breeze in leaves
- **Textures:** soft, springy leaves; warm flagstones; rough bark of the seat
- **Smells:** lavender; rosemary; warm earth
- **Time of day:** late morning
- **Light:** bright, dappled sunlight
- **Way in:** Three shallow stone steps from the garden path.
- **Way out:** The same steps, and an open gate at the far end.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2, 3

### The Orchard Path

A broad, level path under rows of apple and pear trees. Sunlight reaches the grass between the trunks. Ripe fruit sits in a basket at the start of the path, and the way ahead is always clear to see.

- **Sights:** rows of fruit trees; patches of sunlight on grass; a woven basket; a clear path ahead
- **Sounds:** leaves moving; a distant blackbird
- **Textures:** cool, smooth fruit; short, soft grass; packed earth underfoot
- **Smells:** ripe apples; cut grass
- **Time of day:** afternoon
- **Light:** warm light filtering through leaves
- **Way in:** A gate from the Herb Terrace, standing open.
- **Way out:** The gate behind you, and an open lawn at the far end.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2, 3

### The Stepping-Stone Stream

A narrow, clear stream, shallow enough to see every pebble, running over smooth pebbles through the garden. Wide, flat stepping stones cross it, and a mossy bank on either side makes a good place to sit.

- **Sights:** clear water over pebbles; wide flat stones; green moss; small dragonflies
- **Sounds:** gentle trickling water; the odd bird call
- **Textures:** cool, smooth pebbles; soft moss; dry, warm stone
- **Smells:** fresh water; green leaves
- **Time of day:** midday
- **Light:** bright daylight with soft shade
- **Way in:** A grassy track leading from the Orchard Path.
- **Way out:** Either bank leads straight back onto open grass.
- **Suits phases:** 2, 3

### The Lemon Grove

A small, airy grove of lemon trees, planted in a loose circle around a stone bench. The trees are low and the sky is always open overhead. Yellow fruit gleams against the leaves.

## The Harbour Village

A small, sunny harbour with a wooden jetty, open huts and a lamp-lit, level promenade.

### The Sunlit Jetty

A broad wooden jetty reaching a short way over calm, shallow water, wide enough for two people to walk side by side. Boards are warm from the sun, and the water below is clear enough to see the sandy bottom. The jetty is short, and the shore is always close behind.

- **Sights:** sun-bleached boards; clear shallow water; small boats moored nearby; the village behind
- **Sounds:** boards creaking softly; water lapping the posts
- **Textures:** warm, smooth boards; rope handrail
- **Smells:** warm wood; salt
- **Time of day:** late afternoon
- **Light:** golden light
- **Way in:** A wide ramp from the harbour promenade.
- **Way out:** The ramp and the shore, always in view.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2, 3

### The Open Hut

A small hut with a thatched roof and no walls, so it is open on all four sides. Cushions sit on a wide wooden floor. A breeze moves through, and you can see the sea, the garden and the village from where you sit.

- **Sounds:** a soft breeze; distant waves; a wind chime
- **Textures:** soft woven cushions; smooth wooden floor
- **Smells:** dried grass; salt air
- **Time of day:** mid-afternoon
- **Light:** warm, bright and open
- **Way in:** A wide step from the promenade.
- **Way out:** Open on every side, with the promenade a step away.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2
