## The island

A raw, slightly rugged Aegean island: pale limestone, dry-stone walls, pine and olive on scrub-covered slopes, clear turquoise water and long, rich sunsets. Nature dominates. People live here in a few small, old places built into the land, and nothing is polished for visitors.

**Palette:** Pale stone and whitewash, rich pine greens, silvery olive leaves, turquoise shallows and rich blue water further out, warm red earth, and sunset tones of amber, coral, rose and violet.

**Rules for every place:**

- Mediterranean, never tropical: umbrella pines, olives, cypress, juniper, figs, vines and aromatic herbs. No palms, lagoons or white-sand resort beaches.
- Raw rather than manicured: paths are worn, not built; walls are dry stone; paint is faded by salt and sun. Pale rock breaks through the vegetation everywhere.
- Nature leads. Buildings and paths are small, old and fitted to the terrain.
- Never artificial, fantastical or luxury-resort-like. No roads, vehicles, crowds, signs, hotels, nightlife, industry or dense building.
- Human life is slow, social and purposeful: people work, talk and eat together without hurry.
- One consistent geography, climate, light and soundscape, believable enough that a listener can imagine standing there.
- Every place should make it easier to be completely present.

**Themes:** serenity, freedom, awe, release, presence, belonging, perspective, simplicity, connection with nature.

## The Cove

The emotional heart of the island: a gently sheltered bay held by rocky headlands and scrub-covered mountain slopes, with pale sand, clear turquoise water and the open sea behind. It belongs to the sunset.

### The Cove

You stand with your feet just inside the water of a gently sheltered bay. The tiniest waves move across your feet and ankles, refreshing but not cold, still holding the warmth of the day, and the wet sand beneath you is firm, smooth and supportive. Ahead, the land rises in uneven green slopes of pine, juniper and scrub, with pale rock breaking through. The rocky, mountainous sides of the cove curve protectively round you and hide the village and the rest of the coast, so the cove feels private but never closed in. To the left, the sun shines brilliantly above the rocky ridge, still high enough that it has not yet begun to set behind it. Behind you is the open sea, calm and glimmering, stretching on as far as you can see.

- **Sights:** the sun intensely golden above the rocky ridge to the left; a sky turning amber, coral, red, rose, vivid pink, lavender and violet, with a few thin, wispy clouds catching the colour; the nearest mountains taking on copper, rose and violet tones, with a delicate golden haze softening the distant ridges; exceptionally clear turquoise water over a pale, sandy seabed, with only a few scattered rocks and pebbles; small, colourful fish occasionally moving through the shallows; umbrella pines, olives, cypress, juniper and low shrubs on the slopes, with pale rock breaking through; a narrow gap in the green growth at the far left, where the path begins
- **Sounds:** the smallest waves folding onto the shore; faint cicadas further back in the scrub; pine branches moving lightly in the breeze; an occasional distant bird; now and then a fish briefly touching the surface
- **Textures:** small waves moving gently across the feet and ankles; water refreshing but not cold, still warm from the day; firm, smooth wet sand underfoot; a very gentle breeze on the face; clear, crisp air
- **Smells:** clean salt air; pine resin drifting down from the slopes; warm herbs on the breeze
- **Time of day:** sunset, while the sun is still above the ridge
- **Light:** Intensely golden sun with a kaleidoscopic sky; the cove itself stays clear and bright, and the open sea behind glimmers softly in the evening light.
- **Way in:** The old path from the village, which comes out as a narrow gap in the vegetation at the far left of the beach.
- **Way out:** That gap in the green at the far left, and the open, pale sand of the beach all around you.
- **Feeling:** Serenity, presence, liberty and release: nowhere else you would rather be. Not escape through avoidance but freedom through complete presence, part of the landscape: the same breeze that moves the pines touches your face, and the same water that fills the cove moves around your feet. Burdens briefly stop mattering.
- **Suits phases:** 1, 2, 3, 4

## The Pine Path

### The High Plateau
